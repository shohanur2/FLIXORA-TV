## SparkBoxCore

FLIXORA integrates the MovieBox/WeFeed protocol client from
[devfahim00/SparkBoxCore](https://github.com/devfahim00/SparkBoxCore).

- License: MIT
- Upstream revision used by the Android build: `f4bc3c561a3eb723bf9c466159e04aae98f45d3c`
- Native integration: Rust C-ABI library, called from Flutter through Dart FFI.
- Local FLIXORA build configuration sets the MovieBox client region to BD and timezone to Asia/Dhaka.
